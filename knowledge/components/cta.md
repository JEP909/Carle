# Component: CTA section

Its job: convert. One clear ask, made compelling, with nothing competing.

## What it needs
- A **punchy headline** — a final, benefit-led nudge. Often imperative.
- An optional **one-line support** removing the last objection.
- One **primary action**, dominant. At most one quiet secondary.
- Optional reassurance ("Free for 14 days · no card") under the button.

## How to reason about it
- Singular focus: this section should have the least competing content on the
  page. Strip anything that isn't the headline or the button.
- The button is a co-focal point with the headline — give it real presence,
  a confident hover and press state.
- Often visually distinct from surrounding sections (an accent field, a darker
  band, a luminous treatment) to signal "this is the moment."
- Keep copy short and active. No paragraphs.
- Motion: hover lift + glow on the button, press feedback, optional reveal or a
  subtle background motion that doesn't distract from the action.
