# Component: Feature card

Its job: make one product capability tangible and desirable. The most common —
and most often botched — landing section.

## What it needs
- An optional small **eyebrow/label** naming the capability area.
- A **benefit-led headline** — what the user gets, not the mechanism. Lead with
  the outcome.
- A short **supporting line** explaining how, concisely.
- Crucially, a **visual that SHOWS the feature**: a small product mockup, a
  representative UI fragment, a diagram, or an animated detail — not a generic
  icon. The visual is what separates a great feature card from a slop one.
- Optionally a quiet link ("See how it works →").

## How to reason about it
- Decide the focal point: sometimes the visual leads, sometimes the headline.
  Compose accordingly — don't default to "icon on top, text below."
- The visual must be specific and legible at the card's real size. A tiny,
  detailed mockup that reads clearly beats a vague abstract blob.
- Keep the copy tight. One benefit, made vivid. Don't list five things.
- Balance the visual against the text — neither should crowd the other. This is
  the failure mode: a busy mockup fighting cramped copy. Give each its space.
- Motion: a reveal-on-scroll, a hover lift, or a small animated detail in the
  mockup (a cursor, a state change) that demonstrates the feature.
