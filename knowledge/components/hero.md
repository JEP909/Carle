# Component: Hero

The first thing a visitor sees. Its job: in one glance, say what this is and why
it matters, and offer one clear next step.

## What it needs
- A sharp, benefit-led **headline** — the single most important text on the page.
  Specific and concrete, not a vague slogan. This is the focal point.
- A short **supporting line** that adds the crucial detail the headline can't.
- One **primary action** (and at most one quiet secondary). Make the primary
  unmistakable.
- Usually a **visual** — a product shot, an abstract figure, a representative UI,
  or an atmospheric treatment — that reinforces the message. Not a stock cliché.
- Optionally a small trust signal (logos, a rating, a one-line proof).

## How to reason about it
- Hierarchy is everything here: headline → support → action → visual, in that
  order of weight. Don't let the visual fight the headline.
- Give it room. Heroes breathe more than any other section.
- The headline sets the type voice for the whole site — make it count.
- Motion: a restrained reveal-on-load (headline, then support, then action,
  staggered) is the classic premium touch. Optionally a subtle parallax or sheen
  on the visual. Never overdo it.
- Avoid the generic "centered headline + subhead + two pills on a gradient." Let
  the product and register dictate the composition (split, asymmetric, etc.).
