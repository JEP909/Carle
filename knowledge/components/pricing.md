# Component: Pricing card

Its job: present one plan and make choosing it easy and confident.

## What it needs
- The **tier name** (eyebrow or small heading).
- The **price**, with cadence (/mo, /yr) — the focal point, set large.
- A concise **value line**: who this plan is for.
- An itemised **list of what's included**, with crisp affirmative markers (a
  well-drawn check, not an emoji). Keep items short and scannable.
- One **primary CTA**, unmissable.
- Optionally a **"most popular" emphasis** treatment (a badge + a lifted/
  highlighted card) when this is the recommended tier.
- Optional reassurance under the CTA ("No card required · cancel anytime").

## How to reason about it
- The price is the hero. Set it big and tight; make the cadence subordinate.
- The CTA is the second focal point — give it weight and a clear hover state.
- The feature list should scan in under two seconds: align markers, keep lines to
  one row, group logically.
- Differentiate an emphasized plan with elevation, accent, or a badge — not by
  making it garish.
- Avoid the dead-generic centered list template; compose for the plan's story.
- Motion: hover lift on the card, press feedback on the CTA, optional reveal.
