# Component: Testimonial / social proof

Its job: borrow credibility from a real voice. Make the praise feel human and
specific, not marketing fluff.

## What it needs
- The **quote** — the hero. Specific and believable; a concrete result beats
  vague enthusiasm. Set it at a readable, slightly elevated size.
- **Attribution**: name, role, company — and ideally a real-feeling avatar or
  logo. Without attribution it reads as fabricated.
- Optionally a small **proof detail**: a metric, a rating, or a company logo.

## How to reason about it
- The quote leads; attribution is quiet but present. Don't let chrome (giant
  quotation marks, heavy cards) overpower the words.
- Keep the quote to a believable length — a sentence or two that says something
  specific. Avoid generic "This product changed our lives!" copy.
- Treat the avatar/logo with care: crisp, aligned, real-feeling.
- Register matters: enterprise proof is restrained; consumer proof can be warmer.
- Motion: a gentle reveal; optionally a subtle hover on the card. Keep it calm —
  testimonials should feel trustworthy, not flashy.
