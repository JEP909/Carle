# Recipe: Logo Grid on a Wash

Transcribed from the Chatbase omnichannel card (real channel logos in white tiles
on a purple watercolor field). Use when showing several real integrations/channels.

## When to use
"Works across all channels", omnichannel, integrations as a set of named
platforms, app marketplace.

## The construction (exact)
- **Field:** a saturated, slightly grainy watercolor gradient (purple/indigo),
  ~24px radius, soft outer shadow — committed color, not pale.
- **Floating white grid panel** inside the field (~18px radius, layered shadow so
  it floats), holding a 2x2 (or 2x3) grid of **tiles**. Each tile: light-gray
  rounded square, a REAL brand logo ({{logo:slack}}, {{logo:whatsapp}},
  {{logo:messenger}}, {{logo:gmail}}, etc. — never hand-drawn), and the platform
  name beneath in medium-weight ink.
- Keep tiles even, generous padding, real logos at ~28px in their brand colors.

## Reference-grade vs lazy
- GREAT: real logos in their true colors, a floating white grid with depth on a
  confident wash, even tasteful spacing.
- LAZY: hand-drawn glyphs, a pale flat background, tiles with no depth, a generic
  bolt/sparkle anywhere. (No decorative "channels connected" pill — it reads as
  filler.)

Geist; heading + 2-line support below the field.
