# Recipe: Orbit Constellation

Transcribed from the Chatbase "Designed for simplicity" and "Purpose-built for
LLMs" feature cards. Use when the brief is about connecting/orchestrating things,
an AI doing work, or a hub that ties tools together. This is a RICH, playful
diorama — depth and motion-implied, not flat.

## When to use
Integrations, "it connects everything," agent-does-the-work, orchestration,
routing, "powered by many models." If the brief implies a center that relates to
satellites, this is the recipe.

## The construction (exact)
- **Surface:** a white card, ~24px radius, generous padding (~32px), a soft
  multi-layer shadow. The diorama fills the top ~60–65%; copy anchored below.
- **The orbit:** a large **dotted circle** (1.5px dots, low-opacity ink ~12%)
  centered in the upper area, ~280px across. It implies the system's reach.
- **The center object:** a single bold focal mark sitting dead-center of the
  orbit — e.g. a black squircle app icon (~64px, 18px radius) OR a black pill
  button labeled with the core action ("Create agent"). CRITICAL detail: it has
  a **gradient underglow** — a soft coral→magenta→amber blurred bar offset ~10–14px
  below the object, as if lit from beneath. This is the signature; without it the
  center looks flat.
- **Floating satellites around the orbit** (3–5, placed ON the dotted ring at
  varied clock positions, each a small white rounded tile with a soft shadow):
  - a real brand-logo tile ({{logo:...}} — Google, OpenAI, etc.)
  - a tiny formatting toolbar (B / I / U / S in a pill)
  - a cluster of 3 overlapping color dots (green/lime)
  - a small toggle (green, ON)
  - an outlined pill action ("Reply with AI") — a sparkle here IS allowed because
    it's a real product affordance executed cleanly, not a generic hero glyph
- **Dotted connectors:** thin dotted/dashed lines linking the center to a couple
  of satellites (curved feels more crafted than straight).

## What makes it reference-grade vs lazy
- GREAT: real logos, real little UI fragments as satellites, the underglow on the
  center, varied placement around the ring, soft shadows giving each tile lift.
- LAZY (do NOT ship): a single generic bolt/sparkle in the center with no
  underglow; satellites that are blank or generic shapes; a flat ring with no
  depth. If you can't make the satellites real product fragments, use a different
  recipe.

## Palette & type
White surface, near-black ink (#0a0a0a heading), muted gray body. One accent for
the underglow gradient (coral→magenta) — used ONLY there, everything else calm.
Geist. Heading below the scene, tight, weight 600; 2-line muted support.
